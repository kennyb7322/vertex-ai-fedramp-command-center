FedRAMP-authorized regions and data residency

Compute, storage and model calls stay in US locations allowed by the boundary. Calls go to regional Vertex AI endpoints; the global endpoint is out of scope because it does not carry the residency commitment.

Vertex AI

Model enablement and model access permissions

Two layers decide who can call which model: an organization policy that allow-lists models for the whole folder, and IAM that grants the single permission needed to call them. Grounding with Google Search is turned off at the folder.

Folder policy — vertexai.allowedModels

List constraint; anything not listed is denied, including other Gemini versions.

Zero data retention
